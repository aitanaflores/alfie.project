My Top 5 Favorite Book Series

These are my five favorite book series that I love reading.

  1. Dream Harbor Series

    The Dream Harbor series is a cozy romance series filled with small-town charm, love, friendship, and memorable characters. Each book follows a different couple while bringing readers back to the welcoming town of Dream Harbor.

  2. A Series of Unfortunate Events

    This series follows the three Baudelaire siblings as they face mysterious events and unfortunate situations after losing their parents. It is filled with adventure, mystery, clever humor, and unexpected twists.

  3. Neapolitan Novels

    The Neapolitan Novels follow the complicated lifelong friendship between Elena and Lila as they grow up in Naples, Italy. The series explores friendship, family, identity, jealousy, and the challenges of growing up.

  4. Throne of Glass

    Throne of Glass is a fantasy series about Celaena Sardothien, a young assassin who becomes involved in a dangerous world of magic, royalty, and political conflict. The series is filled with action, romance, adventure, and powerful characters.

  5. The Ravenhood Series

    The Ravenhood series is a romantic suspense series filled with secrets, mystery, complicated relationships, and emotional twists. It follows characters navigating love, loyalty, betrayal, and difficult choices.
